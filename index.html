Good to know

Your Weekend Questions

What should I wear?+

Colorful and floral is encouraged. Floral dresses, colorful ties, and shirts are all welcome. September weather can change quickly, so bring a warm layer and outdoor-friendly shoes.

What is the weather plan?+

The ceremony and celebration are outdoors, with a tent available for rain or sun. Washington weather in September can be unpredictable, so we recommend a light jacket or warmer layer.

Where will guests park?+

Parking is available at the cabin. Please plan your transportation ahead of time and travel with the details you need for the evening.

Is the cabin accessible?+

The celebration area has no stairs. There will be on-site bathrooms, with tent coverage and level outdoor paths where possible.

Can I bring a pet?+

We love our animal friends, but pets will not be allowed at the wedding.

Will there be Wi-Fi?+

Yes. Wi-Fi and data service are available at the cabin.

Can I take photos?+

Absolutely. We would love to share favorite moments after the wedding. A photo-upload option will be added here before the weekend.
